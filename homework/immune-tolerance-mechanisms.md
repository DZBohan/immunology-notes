### 1. Why does thymic escape not always cause disease?

Imagine that thymic expression of a self antigen decreases. More T cells recognizing that antigen survive.

- Explain how reduced antigen expression could affect negative selection.
- Describe two peripheral mechanisms that could still prevent disease.

### 2. What causes a low T-cell response?

Imagine that T cells produce little IL-2 after stimulation.

- Explain how anergy, deletion, and Treg suppression could each cause this result.
- Propose two measurements or comparisons that distinguish these mechanisms.

### 3. How would you test a Treg mechanism?

Imagine that adding Tregs reduces APC surface CD80/CD86 and T-cell proliferation.

- Design an experiment to test whether Treg CTLA-4 contributes.
- Include a control, your measurements, and your predicted results.

### 4. How can tissue injury sustain autoimmunity?

- Explain how injury could promote responses to additional self epitopes. Connect your explanation to epitope spreading.
- Describe two mechanisms that can still control self-reactive B cells.

### Submission

Answer all four questions in up to 1,000 words. Tables and diagrams are welcome. Explain your reasoning and the limits of your evidence.
