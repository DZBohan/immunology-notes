### Background

Scientists hypothesize that the strong innate and adaptive immune responses triggered by viral infection may sometimes enhance antitumor immunity. ## Bohan's answer

### 1. Infection and antitumor immune responses

SARS-CoV-2 infection can activate innate immunity through pattern recognition receptors, induce cytokine production, enhance natural killer cell function, and promote adaptive immunity through dendritic cells. Under conditions such as effective tumor antigen presentation, these responses may enhance tumor cell recognition and clearance. However, tumor regression after infection in some patients does not establish causation or justify using COVID-19 as a cancer treatment.

#### 1) PRRs

SARS-CoV-2 infection involves Toll-like receptors TLR7/8 and RIG-I-like receptors MDA5 and RIG-I. TLR7/8 signal mainly through the adaptor protein MyD88. MDA5 and RIG-I can signal through MAVS. These pathways activate transcription factors such as IRFs and NF-κB, initiating antiviral and inflammatory responses.

#### 2) PAMPs

TLR7 and TLR8 recognize processed viral single-stranded RNA fragments in endosomes. MDA5 mainly detects long double-stranded RNA formed during viral replication. RIG-I can detect virus-associated RNA with suitable terminal or double-stranded structures. SARS-CoV-2 has a single-stranded RNA genome, but infection can still generate structures recognized by double-stranded RNA sensors.

#### 3) Cytokines

SARS-CoV-2 infection can induce type I interferons IFN-α and IFN-β and type III interferon IFN-λ to help establish an antiviral state. Infection and resulting tissue damage can promote proinflammatory cytokine production, including TNF-α, IL-6, and IL-1β. Chemokines such as CXCL10 and CXCL8 help recruit immune cells to affected tissues. Activated NK and T cells can also produce IFN-γ as the immune response develops. Interferon and inflammatory responses vary with infection stage and severity.

#### 4) Roles of type I interferons, TNF-α, and IL-6

Type I interferons mainly limit viral spread by establishing an antiviral state. IFN-α and IFN-β bind cell-surface IFNAR, activate JAK–STAT signaling, and induce interferon-stimulated gene expression. TNF-α mainly promotes inflammation, vascular endothelial activation, and leukocyte recruitment. It increases endothelial adhesion molecule expression, facilitating immune cell entry from blood into infected or damaged tissues. IL-6 activates pathways including JAK–STAT3 through IL-6R and gp130 to coordinate acute-phase responses and immune cell function. It stimulates hepatocytes to produce acute-phase proteins and contributes to B-cell antibody responses and T-cell differentiation.

#### 5) NK cells

NK cells integrate activating and inhibitory signals to determine whether to kill target cells. Some tumor cells reduce MHC-I expression, weakening NK cell inhibition. Stress-induced ligands on tumor cells may also enhance NK cell activation. Infection-induced cytokines, including type I interferons, can further enhance NK cell cytotoxicity. Activated NK cells release perforin and granzymes to induce target cell death. They also secrete IFN-γ to help regulate other immune cells.

#### 6) Dendritic cells and CD8 T cells

Dendritic cells take up tumor antigens and can cross-present them on MHC-I to antigen-specific CD8 T cells. Effective activation requires TCR recognition of peptide–MHC-I complexes, costimulation, and cytokine signals supporting T-cell expansion and differentiation. Activated tumor-specific CD8 T cells can then migrate to tumors, recognize cells expressing the corresponding antigens, and kill them through mechanisms including perforin and granzymes. Some may become memory cells and support subsequent immune surveillance.

### 2. Cancer immunotherapy design

Deliver a TLR7/8 agonist without live virus locally to the tumor and investigate its effects in combination with anti-PD-1 therapy. This mimics virus-induced innate immune stimulation to create a local environment that supports immune recognition and tumor clearance. Adding anti-PD-1 therapy aims to relieve inhibitory signaling in T cells and improve their contribution to tumor clearance.

Compare tumor growth across control, TLR7/8 agonist alone, anti-PD-1 alone, and combination groups. Assess dendritic cell activation and NK and CD8 T-cell infiltration and effector function. Cell depletion experiments in animal models can test whether treatment efficacy depends on NK or CD8 T cells. Also monitor systemic cytokine levels and tissue damage. Local delivery aims to limit systemic exposure but cannot eliminate inflammatory toxicity.
