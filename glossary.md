# Glossary

Immunology is an acronym swamp, so the terms live outside the notes. When adding
one, write **what it does**, not just what it expands to. Chinese is kept here
deliberately — terminology is where the second language earns its place.

| Acronym | Expansion | Chinese | What it does |
|---------|-----------|---------|--------------|
| DC | Dendritic Cell | 树突状细胞 | Carries antigen to lymph nodes; the bridge from innate to adaptive immunity |
| NK | Natural Killer cell | 自然杀伤细胞 | Kills virus-infected and tumour cells; innate, despite lymphocyte morphology |
| PAMP | Pathogen-Associated Molecular Pattern | 病原体相关分子模式 | Molecules common to a pathogen class and absent from host cells — what innate immunity actually recognises |
| DAMP | Damage-Associated Molecular Pattern | 损伤相关分子模式 | Host molecules released by damaged cells; also triggers innate responses |
| PRR | Pattern Recognition Receptor | 模式识别受体 | The receptors that detect PAMPs and DAMPs; germline-encoded, so no lead time |
| TLR | Toll-like Receptor | Toll 样受体 | The best-known PRR family |
| MHC | Major Histocompatibility Complex | 主要组织相容性复合体 | Presents peptides to T cells. Called **HLA** in humans — same thing, two names |
| TCR | T Cell Receptor | T 细胞受体 | The antigen receptor of T cells; somatically rearranged |
| BCR | B Cell Receptor | B 细胞受体 | The antigen receptor of B cells; membrane-bound antibody |
| RIG-I | Retinoic Acid-Inducible Gene I | 视黄酸诱导基因 I | Cytosolic sensor of viral RNA that signals through MAVS |
| MDA5 | Melanoma Differentiation-Associated Protein 5 | 黑色素瘤分化相关蛋白 5 | Cytosolic viral-RNA sensor; particularly important for long double-stranded RNA |
| MAVS | Mitochondrial Antiviral Signalling Protein | 线粒体抗病毒信号蛋白 | Mitochondrial adaptor that relays signals from RIG-I and MDA5 to IRF3 and NF-κB |
| TLR3 | Toll-Like Receptor 3 | Toll 样受体 3 | Endosomal receptor that detects double-stranded RNA and signals through TRIF |
| TRIF | TIR-Domain-Containing Adapter-Inducing Interferon-β | 诱导干扰素-β的 TIR 域衔接蛋白 | Adaptor linking TLR3 to IRF3 and NF-κB activation |
| IRF3 | Interferon Regulatory Factor 3 | 干扰素调节因子 3 | Transcription factor that drives interferon production after viral sensing |
| NF-κB | Nuclear Factor Kappa B | 核因子 κB | Transcription factor that induces inflammatory genes |
| IFN | Interferon | 干扰素 | Cytokine that warns infected and neighbouring cells and induces an antiviral programme |
| JAK | Janus Kinase | Janus 激酶 | Receptor-associated kinase that transmits interferon signals to STAT proteins |
| STAT | Signal Transducer and Activator of Transcription | 信号转导及转录激活蛋白 | Transcription factor activated downstream of cytokine receptors, including IFN receptors |
| ISG | Interferon-Stimulated Gene | 干扰素刺激基因 | One of the genes induced by interferon signalling that helps establish an antiviral state |
| NLR | NOD-Like Receptor | NOD 样受体 | Cytosolic pattern-recognition receptor family that detects microbial products or cellular stress |
| RLR | RIG-I-Like Receptor | RIG-I 样受体 | Cytosolic viral-RNA sensor family that includes RIG-I and MDA5 |
| CLR | C-Type Lectin Receptor | C 型凝集素受体 | Recognises carbohydrate structures, especially on fungi and other microbes |
| CDS | Cytosolic DNA Sensor | 胞质 DNA 感受器 | Umbrella term for sensors that detect DNA misplaced in the cytosol; not a single receptor family |
| TNF | Tumour Necrosis Factor | 肿瘤坏死因子 | Pro-inflammatory cytokine that activates endothelium and helps coordinate local and systemic inflammation |
| IL | Interleukin | 白细胞介素 | Cytokine naming family; the number identifies a molecule but does not predict its function |
| BCG | Bacillus Calmette–Guérin | 卡介苗 | Tuberculosis vaccine that can also induce broad, nonspecific changes associated with trained immunity |
| mtDNA | Mitochondrial DNA | 线粒体 DNA | Normally contained within mitochondria; extracellular or cytosolic mtDNA can act as a danger signal |
| HMGB1 | High-Mobility Group Box 1 | 高迁移率族蛋白 B1 | Nuclear protein that acts as a DAMP when released by damaged cells |
| ATP | Adenosine Triphosphate | 三磷酸腺苷 | Intracellular energy carrier that acts as an extracellular danger signal after tissue damage |
| ROS | Reactive Oxygen Species | 活性氧 | Reactive molecules used in microbial killing but capable of damaging host tissue when excessive |
| NET | Neutrophil Extracellular Trap | 中性粒细胞胞外捕获网 | DNA–protein structure released by neutrophils to trap microbes; excessive formation can injure tissue |
| ssRNA | Single-Stranded RNA | 单链 RNA | Viral nucleic-acid pattern detected by selected endosomal and cytosolic receptors |
| dsRNA | Double-Stranded RNA | 双链 RNA | Viral replication-associated pattern detected by TLR3, RIG-I, or MDA5, depending on its length and location |
| CpG | Cytosine–Phosphate–Guanine | CpG 二核苷酸 | Unmethylated CpG-rich DNA can signal microbial DNA to innate receptors |
| LPS | Lipopolysaccharide | 脂多糖 | Gram-negative bacterial outer-membrane component recognised through the TLR4–CD14 system |
| LTA | Lipoteichoic Acid | 脂磷壁酸 | Gram-positive bacterial cell-envelope component commonly recognised through TLR2-containing receptor complexes |
| CD14 | Cluster of Differentiation 14 | 分化簇 14 | Co-receptor that helps deliver LPS to the TLR4 signalling complex |
| GPI | Glycosylphosphatidylinositol | 糖基磷脂酰肌醇 | Membrane anchor found in eukaryotes; some protozoal GPI anchors act as PAMPs |
| F-actin | Filamentous Actin | 丝状肌动蛋白 | Polymerised cytoskeletal actin that can act as a DAMP when exposed or released by damaged cells |
| S100A8/A9 | S100 Calcium-Binding Proteins A8 and A9 | S100 钙结合蛋白 A8/A9 | Stress-released alarmins that often form the calprotectin complex and amplify inflammation |
| NOD1/2 | Nucleotide-Binding Oligomerisation Domain-Containing Proteins 1 and 2 | 核苷酸结合寡聚化结构域蛋白 1/2 | Cytosolic NLRs that detect distinct fragments of bacterial peptidoglycan |
| NLRP | NLR Family Pyrin Domain-Containing | 含热蛋白结构域的 NLR 家族 | Sensor family whose activated members can assemble inflammasomes |
| AIM2 | Absent in Melanoma 2 | 黑色素瘤缺失因子 2 | Cytosolic double-stranded-DNA sensor that can assemble an inflammasome |
| STING | Stimulator of Interferon Genes | 干扰素基因刺激蛋白 | Endoplasmic-reticulum adaptor that relays cytosolic DNA-sensing signals to interferon production |
| DC-SIGN | Dendritic Cell-Specific ICAM-3-Grabbing Non-Integrin | 树突状细胞特异性 ICAM-3 结合非整合素 | C-type lectin receptor that binds mannose- or fucose-containing microbial glycans |
| CD36 | Cluster of Differentiation 36 | 分化簇 36 | Scavenger receptor that binds selected microbial and altered host lipids |
| CRP | C-Reactive Protein | C 反应蛋白 | Soluble pentraxin that binds selected microbial or damaged-cell surfaces and promotes clearance |
| MBL | Mannose-Binding Lectin | 甘露糖结合凝集素 | Soluble collectin that recognises microbial carbohydrates and can initiate the lectin complement pathway |
| SP-A/D | Surfactant Proteins A and D | 肺表面活性蛋白 A/D | Alveolar collectins that bind microbial structures and support pulmonary host defence |
| MyD88 | Myeloid Differentiation Primary Response 88 | 髓样分化主要应答蛋白 88 | Adaptor used by most TLRs to activate inflammatory signalling |
| RIPK2 | Receptor-Interacting Serine/Threonine-Protein Kinase 2 | 受体相互作用丝氨酸/苏氨酸蛋白激酶 2 | Kinase adaptor linking NOD1/2 recognition to downstream inflammatory pathways; also called RIP2 |
| CARD9 | Caspase Recruitment Domain-Containing Protein 9 | 含半胱氨酸天冬氨酸蛋白酶募集结构域蛋白 9 | Adaptor that relays signals from several myeloid C-type lectin receptors |
| BCL10 | B-Cell Lymphoma/Leukaemia 10 | B 细胞淋巴瘤/白血病蛋白 10 | Signalling scaffold that works with CARD9 downstream of selected C-type lectin receptors |
| PTX3 | Pentraxin 3 | 长链五聚蛋白 3 | Soluble pattern-recognition molecule that binds selected microbes and supports opsonisation |
| LBP | Lipopolysaccharide-Binding Protein | 脂多糖结合蛋白 | Soluble protein that captures LPS and transfers it to CD14 |
| MD-2 | Myeloid Differentiation Factor 2 | 髓样分化因子 2 | TLR4-associated protein that binds the lipid A portion of LPS and enables receptor activation |
| TIRAP | TIR Domain-Containing Adaptor Protein | 含 TIR 结构域的衔接蛋白 | Bridging adaptor that recruits MyD88 to selected plasma-membrane TLRs, including TLR2 and TLR4 |
| TRAM | TRIF-Related Adaptor Molecule | TRIF 相关衔接分子 | Adaptor that connects endosomal TLR4 to TRIF-dependent signalling |
| AP-1 | Activator Protein 1 | 激活蛋白 1 | Transcription-factor complex that induces inflammatory and stress-response genes |

## Naming conventions worth knowing

Saves a lot of pointless memorisation:

- **CD** — Cluster of Differentiation, the unified numbering scheme for cell
  surface molecules. **The number carries no meaning**; it reflects the order of
  discovery. Do not look for a pattern.
- **IL** — Interleukin, numbered the same way: by order of discovery.
- **MHC / HLA** — the same complex under two names. MHC is the general term;
  HLA is the human one.
